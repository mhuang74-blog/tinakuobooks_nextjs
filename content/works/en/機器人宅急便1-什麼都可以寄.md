At her old job, Bala took more time over each package than her boss liked. Now she and Billy have a place of their own, where they can say yes to some unusual requests. The first is a great slab of ice meant for a polar bear. Even the seabirds get mixed up in the delivery.

Draw the route as Bala and Billy make their way towards the polar bear. Mark the seabirds and the snow as they come up, then let a child add one thing the robots could have packed. What would that one item change about the trip? There’s room for several good answers before you turn the page.

As you read, let children help the robots plan the trip. Snow makes it harder to get where they’re going. Could it be good for the ice?
