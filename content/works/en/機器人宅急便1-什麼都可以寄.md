In 2099 humans leave everything to robots. Courier Bala is thrown out by her boss for being too thorough and too slow, so she and the robot Billy open their own "we deliver anything" shop. Their first customer wants an enormous block of ice delivered to a polar bear, and the pair must beat melting, a blizzard, and some meddling seabirds to finish the job.

Meet the two partners in this early chapter book for grades 1–4, the first of two in 機器人宅急便.
