Not to be outdone by a classmate's bragging, Ding Xiaofei claims he owns a globally limited-edition pair of glasses and starts inventing alibis for them. As the lies snowball he is nicknamed "Ding the Puppet," and when a TV contest's prize money disappears he becomes the prime suspect. The fourth book is about lying and owning up to it.

In book 4 of the five-volume 丁小飛校園日記 series, a novel for grades 3–4, a lie leaves Ding Xiaofei cornered.
