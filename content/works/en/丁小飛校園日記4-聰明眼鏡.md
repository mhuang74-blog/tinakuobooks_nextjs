Hearing a classmate brag about owning a pair of globally limited-edition glasses, Ding Xiaofei blurts out, "I have a pair too!" To cover up the truth that he lied, Ding Xiaofei starts inventing alibis for the glasses. As the lies pile up and the truth comes out, everyone starts looking at Xiaofei differently and calls him "Ding the Puppet."

Being treated like a rat crossing the street feels awful — and worse, when Ding Xiaofei and his classmates take part in a TV contest, the champion's prize money disappears into thin air! When Ding Xiaofei is suspected of stealing the money to buy limited-edition glasses, how will he clean up this farce that keeps getting more ridiculous?

The funniest survival handbook for a little awkward boy, the most agonizing tug-of-war in his mind — help Ding Xiaofei escape the snowballing lies and face his own feelings honestly!
