> Everyone has times when they are different from everybody else — very different. Dressing differently; eating differently; speaking differently; thinking differently. But if that "different" is something you like and others don't, what will you do?

Little Leopard No. 57 is one of the leopards in the zoo. It doesn't want to look too special, because the leopards around it frighten it: "Humans like cheetahs to look fierce. If you don't look the way humans expect, you'll be made into clothes or a scarf…"

From then on, to keep from being found out as different, Little Leopard No. 57 imitates the others with all its might — it even digs a hole to hide itself. Unexpectedly, it digs a tunnel that leads outside the zoo…
