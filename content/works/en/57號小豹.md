Leopard No. 57 in the zoo doesn't want to look too special, because the others warn it that humans only like fierce-looking cats; anything else gets made into clothes or scarves. It copies the other leopards and even digs a hole to hide in. A picture book about self-acceptance and the companions who offer it.

In this picture book for preschool–grade 2, you can talk about what makes the leopard want to hide each time.
