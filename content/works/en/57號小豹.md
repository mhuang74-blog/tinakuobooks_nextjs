No. 57 watches the other leopards and tries to copy them. It seems like a way to stay out of trouble, especially with the story going around that a leopard who doesn’t look fierce might end up as a coat or scarf. After hearing that, even being out where the others can see feels uncomfortable.

Some children will be worried for the little leopard straight away. Others will be busy trying to spot it on the page. Either can be a place to start. Before suggesting what No. 57 ought to do, imagine another leopard making a bit of room. Would the cub still want to hide? The pictures leave room to wonder what the others could do, too.
