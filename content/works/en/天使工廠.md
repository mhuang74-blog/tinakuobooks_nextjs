On the other side of the world is a world of turtles, where a turtle named Nomen is unhappy at work, on the walk, and at meals. Then a letter from the "Angel Factory" arrives and things begin to change. A picture book about imagination and kindness, with English text alongside the Chinese.

This picture book for preschool–grade 2 to read together makes room to pause at Nomen’s troubles and talk about your own feelings.
