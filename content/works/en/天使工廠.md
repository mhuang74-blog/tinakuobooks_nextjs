Nomen the turtle isn’t keen on going to work or even sitting down to eat. One day goes much like the last until a letter arrives from a place called the Angel Factory. If you’re reading aloud, pause before the letter is opened. Who might have written to Nomen, and what would they want to say?

Afterwards, reverse the game and write a letter to another turtle. It can be just a line long, or a drawing of something the other turtle would recognise. Leave room for children to choose their own small way of being kind.

Tina Kuo wrote and illustrated the book, which has Chinese and English text side by side. An e-book has been published. Print availability is unconfirmed.
