After their "we deliver anything" courier shop becomes locally famous, Bala and Billy are ordered by a robot inspector to fix a compliance problem within three days, or the shop closes and both are shipped back to the factory. Just then a girl walks in wanting to send "courage" to her ninety-six-year-old grandmother. Working out how to deliver something invisible becomes their only way to keep the shop open.

Readers in grades 1–4 can follow the delivery in this early chapter book, the second of two in 機器人宅急便.
