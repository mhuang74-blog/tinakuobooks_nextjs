Detective work runs in Hu Abu’s family. But when the chimpanzee who governs the land goes missing, it’s more than one fox can handle. A willow ptarmigan, a salamander and a vulture who flies a plane all get involved. Who can go where to look for clues is part of the fun when the investigators are animals.

A salamander can follow a lead into the water. A bird can make short work of distances that would stop a fox. Before the team moves on, try giving each investigator a job. A good reason for sending someone can be as much fun to argue over as the identity of a suspect.

Hu Abu has already had cases in both Animal Planet Detective Files collections. Here he has three linked stories of his own. When characters disagree about what happened, consider how the same event might look from the land and from the sea.
