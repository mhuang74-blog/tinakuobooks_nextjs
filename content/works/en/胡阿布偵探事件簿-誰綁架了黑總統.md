After humans abandon Earth, red fox Hu Abu inherits an eight-generation line of detectives and finally lands the case that could make his name: the chimpanzee president who governs the land has vanished. He sets out with a willow ptarmigan and an amphibious salamander investigator, flying on a vulture-piloted presidential plane into affairs that span land, sea, and sky. Three linked stories, each hiding a harder choice than the mystery itself.

This novel for grades 3–4 (ages 10–12) is the only volume to date in the 胡阿布偵探事件簿 series.
