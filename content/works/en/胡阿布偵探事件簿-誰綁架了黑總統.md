After humans abandon Earth, red fox Hu Abu inherits an eight-generation line of detectives and finally
lands the case that could make his name: the chimpanzee president who governs the land has vanished.
He sets out with a willow ptarmigan and an amphibious salamander investigator, flying on a vulture-piloted
presidential plane into affairs that span land, sea, and sky.
Three linked stories, each hiding a harder choice than the mystery itself.
