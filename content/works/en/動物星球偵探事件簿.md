In “Are Humans Still on Earth?” a fox detective has to ask something most of us never need to think about. If people have been gone for years, where would you look for traces of us? This is Hu Abu’s first appearance. He has more cases in the second anthology and in his own collection.

You can dip in and out of this volume and the second one. Readers who follow Hu Abu into his own book will already have some sense of what Earth looks like to a fox who inherited being a detective from his family.

The writers give animals very different parts in their mysteries. Pick two stories and ask whether the detective could follow the same leads if the animals were taken out. One story involves cruelty to dogs.
