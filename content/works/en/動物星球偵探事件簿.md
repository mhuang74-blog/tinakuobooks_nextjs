Six authors write six detective stories in which an animal plays the decisive role, ranging from a strange wailing inside a tin shack and a string of dog killings to a Shang-dynasty bronze and a forest bat. Tina Kuo's "Smartian, Are You Still on Earth?" sends the fox detective Hu Abu after traces of humans long gone from the planet.

The first of two volumes in 動物星球偵探事件簿 is an anthology for readers in grade 5 and up.

The six stories stand alone and can be read in any order.
