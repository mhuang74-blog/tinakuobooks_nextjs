On the way, Shubi meets a monkey, a blue elephant and a reddish-orange crab. It’s a story you can take slowly. Let children see who’s there before you read on. How might the same invitation sound when you say it to somebody new?

On a second reading, let the child say Shubi’s line while the grown-up does the rest, then switch roles at the next animal. Try saying the big question quietly, as though it took some courage, and then as easily as you’d say hello.

Tina Kuo wrote and illustrated the book. The original came with Chinese and English text, zhuyin pronunciation guides and a bilingual story CD.
