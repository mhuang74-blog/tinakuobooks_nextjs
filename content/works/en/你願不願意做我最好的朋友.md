A puppy named Shubi sets out to find a best friend and meets a monkey, a blue elephant, and an
orange-red crab along the way.
The picture book uses a simple story to ask what a "real" best friend is, with Chinese-English
parallel text, zhuyin, and a bilingual story CD.
