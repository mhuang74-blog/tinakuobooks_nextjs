A puppy named Shubi sets out to find a best friend and meets a monkey, a blue elephant, and an orange-red crab along the way. The picture book uses a simple story to ask what a "real" best friend is, with Chinese-English parallel text, zhuyin, and a bilingual story CD.

This picture book is for preschool–grade 2 to read together, with a chance to pause and talk at each new encounter.
