Seven authors each contribute a detective story in which an animal plays the decisive role: a missing key, a spell sealed since the Shang dynasty, a campus haunting caught on a livestream, clues hidden in Li Bai's poems, a missing pet with a second case behind it. Tina Kuo's "The Mysterious Spiral Bread" has the case-starved fox detective Hu Abu reopen as a baker. Every story stands on its own.

Readers in grade 5 and up who enjoy connecting clues can continue with this anthology, volume 2 of 2 in 動物星球偵探事件簿.
