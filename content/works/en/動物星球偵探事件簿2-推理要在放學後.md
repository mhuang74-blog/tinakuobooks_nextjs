> After school, the deduction begins! A detective story collection full of the unbelievable and the surprising. Seven popular authors show off their skills, setting up puzzle after puzzle — waiting for you to solve them!

* The keys are missing again? Time to call in the "Wildcat Intelligence Network" for a large-scale search.

* A spell sealed since the Shang dynasty is about to be unleashed in full, and the only way to stop it is to find an ancient jade in the National Palace Museum!

* An elementary schooler on social media who calls herself the "Girl of Spirit Words" claims she will protect the campus. What is the truth behind the campus hauntings?

* Eleven-year-old detective boy Mai Qi, relying on his innate intuition, sniffs out a hidden case behind a missing pet…

* Great Immortal Ape takes on a case, teasing out clues from Li Bai's poems such as "Quiet Night Thoughts," "Bring in the Wine," and "Ballad of Changgan."

* Fox detective Hu Abu starts a livestream, but still can't get a single case. Will the day ever come when he turns things around?

* An intimidatingly fierce transfer student beauty claims she is going to pinch one hundred classmates' cheeks, and the exorcist detective decides to accept the challenge.
