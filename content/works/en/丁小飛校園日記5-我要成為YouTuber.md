When classmates start their own livestream channels, Ding Xiaofei sets up a camera too, only to find
that running a show is far harder than eating or sleeping.
He has to pick a channel theme, find a producer, design every episode, and deal with his older brother
A-Da, who has abruptly become an "idiom prophecy master."
The fifth book turns a livestream beginner's fumbling into a school survival guide.
