Ding Xiaofei used to put the big events of his life in a diary. In book five, he’s ready to put himself on camera. His classmates are livestreaming, so why shouldn’t he? Then he has to come up with a show and find someone to help make it. Meanwhile, his older brother A-Da is busy making predictions using Chinese idioms.

You can leave an embarrassing story in a diary. On a channel, Xiaofei has to decide what someone else might want to watch. Put the first and fifth books next to each other and compare the two kinds of record he wants to make. How does imagining an audience change the moment he chooses to share?

Try pitching Ding Xiaofei an episode. What would he make if he were making it for just one friend?
