Future Leaders Summer Camp sounds like a place where people might finally remember Ding Xiaofei’s name. Once he arrives, he discovers everyone else is good at something too. When they put on a play, he does get a part. As for how much glory comes with it, you’d have to ask the horse.

What makes the camp interesting is seeing the same people get very different jobs. He Li-Luo is often the first to speak up. Mao Xiaogua has an especially good memory. The person you would pick at once for one event might have to ask a teammate for help in the next. Watch what Xiaofei does when that happens. What does he care about more at that point, the way his team is doing or the way he looks to the team?

As for the part of the horse, reading it as a defeat takes some of the fun out of the play. Imagine watching from a different place, perhaps from the wings instead of the audience. If you cut the horse from the show altogether, what would somebody else have to do to make the scene work?

Start a conversation with the play. There are people you notice straight away and people who keep things going offstage. If everyone at camp got a vote for team leader, would they be looking for the same thing?
