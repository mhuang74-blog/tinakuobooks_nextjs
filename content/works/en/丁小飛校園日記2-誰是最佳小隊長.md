Ding Xiaofei signs up for a Future Leaders Summer Camp expecting to be the center of attention, then meets rivals who outclass him at every turn and ends up cast as "a horse" in the class play. Unwilling to surrender the spotlight, he keeps looking for a way back. The second book is about competition and refusing to quit.

In the second of five 丁小飛校園日記 novels, for grades 3–4, the action moves to summer camp.
