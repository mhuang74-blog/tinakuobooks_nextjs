> Who is the best team leader?
> Ding Xiaofei, number one in self-confidence, has his standing shaken once again!!
> The Future Leaders Summer Camp is full of hidden tigers and crouching dragons — experts lie in ambush everywhere!
> When faced with someone who steals the spotlight from you, are you willing to just bow down and concede?

To highlight how extraordinary he is, Ding Xiaofei signs up for the "Future Leaders Summer Camp." He assumes he will be the center of attention, only to find that there is always a taller mountain! He Li-Luo, who always raises a hand to grab the floor; the glasses hero who loves drawing conclusions; Fang Lili, who is always bursting with energy; Mao Xiaogua, with his amazing memory; and Qian Liangyong, the school's model-student representative… everyone gives it their all and strives to shine! Leadership training sessions come one after another, and the experts trade move after move. Ding Xiaofei somehow sinks so low that he is cast in the class play as… "a horse"? With his leading-man status about to slip away, how will Ding Xiaofei break through the siege and win back his spotlight?

The funniest survival handbook for a little awkward boy, the most exciting fight to be the main character — never give up until the very last moment!
