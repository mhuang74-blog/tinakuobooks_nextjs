> The funniest guide to getting along with people — remake yourself together with Ding Xiaofei!

Ding Xiaofei is in fourth grade at Mengya Elementary, and he has confidence and dreams unlike anyone else's. His greatest wish is to become "a famous person."

Then one day his extraordinary confidence takes a serious blow: class president Cheng Youshen draws a smiley face in response to the book report he wrote. Suddenly he feels hot all over, as if he were sitting in a hot-air balloon and a breeze were slowly carrying him up into the sky… But… isn't he pretty far from the "vampire prince" type that Cheng Youshen likes? Worse still, his number-one enemy appears — vice class president Jin Sigao joins the battle! When a rival who outclasses him at every turn throws down the gauntlet, how will Ding Xiaofei, who starts out behind, respond? And what even more impressive moves will he need to win Cheng Youshen's admiration?

The funniest guide to getting along with people, the most exciting battle to defend one's honor — whether this little awkward boy's transformation succeeds all comes down to this!
