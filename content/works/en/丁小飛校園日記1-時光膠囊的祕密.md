The school asks everyone to bury a diary in a time capsule, to be opened fifty years later. Ding Xiaofei's secret entry: the girl he likes admires someone else most, so he launches a campaign to defend his reputation and win her over. The first book introduces this over-confident fourth grader.

Readers in grades 3–4 can start with this novel, the first of five in 丁小飛校園日記.
