Junior-high student Lanya sees a divorce agreement on his father's desk and his world collapses. His friends Chicken Drumstick Hero and Qiqi skip school with him and set out to save his parents' marriage. The novel reads everyday gestures as "secret codes of love," and the three kids follow clues that heal them along the way.

In this novel for ages 12+, follow Lanya as she tries to work out what the grown-ups have left unsaid.
