> This is a novel that heals adults and children alike, and its story circles around a junior-high boy named Lanya. Ever since he sees the divorce agreement on his father's desk, his world collapses in an instant; fortunately his good friends Chicken Drumstick Hero and Qiqi step forward and accompany him on a journey to try to save his parents' marriage.

The whole novel is about the secret code of love. On life's journey, everyone has something that needs healing — all those scrapes and bruises, big and small, and the scars inside that cannot be stitched shut all need the secret code of love to heal them.

When you feel helpless or confused, follow the hero of this book and search together — grab hold of the secret code of love! You will find the courage not to let those sad things be equated with your future, and to use the memory of love to choose love, to choose happiness.

> "Let me tell you, Xiaoya, don't be afraid. It's fine — it's just a piece of paper. It hasn't been through the judge's big gavel yet, which means there's still hope, understand?"
> That's right! One sentence from Chicken Drumstick's mom pops the big balloon on my shoulders and suddenly the world is filled with air again.
