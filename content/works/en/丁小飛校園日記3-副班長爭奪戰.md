> To defend his standing, Ding Xiaofei goes to war!
> The title of vice class president vs. a good friend — which one matters more?
> The most moving tug-of-war over friendship — protect what is most precious together with Ding Xiaofei!

They say famous people were all "class officers" when they were young, so Ding Xiaofei resolves to fight for the post of vice class president. (The class president has to have good grades; Ding Xiaofei knows he doesn't qualify, so he eliminates that option outright.) But then a dark horse charges in from out of nowhere — his good friend He Li-Luo is not only nominated, but ends up winning by a landslide!!

Jealousy wraps around Ding Xiaofei like fog, and He Li-Luo looks more and more unbearable. Without playing a few tricks, how can he protect the honor of being vice class president? A string of unlucky incidents follows, and He Li-Luo never suspects a thing — never realizing it is his good friend's "unintentional mistake." Only, something inside Ding Xiaofei starts to feel off… is that a feeling of guilt? But for someone destined to do great things one day, should he lower himself and ask for forgiveness first?

The funniest survival handbook for a little awkward boy, the most thrilling tug-of-war with his conscience — help Ding Xiaofei hold back his temper and make the most sensible decision!
