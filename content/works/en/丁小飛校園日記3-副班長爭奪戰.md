Convinced that famous people were all class officers as kids, Ding Xiaofei runs for vice class president, ruling out class president because it takes good grades. His friend He Li-Luo is nominated instead and wins by a landslide, and jealousy pushes Ding Xiaofei into small sabotages that bring a string of bad luck down on his friend. The third book is a tug-of-war with conscience and a repair job on friendship.

This novel for grades 3–4 is book 3 of 5 in 丁小飛校園日記.
