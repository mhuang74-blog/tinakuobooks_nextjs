> One day, to avoid having to give a speech on stage at the school anniversary, older brother A-Bai hides at home and accidentally stumbles into a black hole underneath the sofa.
> To find his brother A-Bai, younger brother A-Ninggu decides to board the Sofa Spaceship with Dad and the dog Duanduan and venture into the alien world of the black hole to see what is there…

The story is adapted from writer Luo Yijun's original text *Little Son*.

*Little Son* collects the everyday trifles of teasing and horseplay between father and son that writer Luo Yijun posts on Facebook. These small things on Facebook are like objects that pass in an instant in the flow of life; the stacking of each fragment is the presentation of a complete, continuous surface of life. Most of the time it is repetition and turning over again and again, circling the same people and things. But the reason life has vitality is that we must face it without tiring of it, collide with it, and through constant stimulation strengthen the quality of our own souls.

The story is modeled on writer Luo Yijun's family anecdotes: a muddle-headed dad, a graceful and refined mom, a shy and serious older son A-Bai, a clever younger son A-Ninggu who often goes off-script, and Duanduan, the little yellow dog who grows up alongside the family. Their life is sometimes hilarious, sometimes warm, sometimes full of conflict — but it always brings a knowing smile. Isn't that what family is? Though they stumble now and then, they always do their best to heal each other and grow together.
