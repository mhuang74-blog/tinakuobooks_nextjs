Older brother A-Bai hides at home to dodge a school-anniversary speech and stumbles into a black hole under the sofa. Younger brother A-Ninggu boards the Sofa Spaceship with Dad and the dog Duanduan and heads into the black hole's other world to bring him back. Adapted from Luo Yijun's Little Son, the musical was structured by Tina Kuo, written by Lin Menghuan and Zhang Yuan, and produced by AM Creative.

A family argument takes the stage in this musical for family audiences.
