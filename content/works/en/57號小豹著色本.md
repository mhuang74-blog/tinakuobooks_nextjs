Sometimes you finish a book and still want to spend a little time with its character. My little sister drew a few pages based on No. 57, and I put them together as a printable colouring book. Start with whichever page your child likes. If you colour one too, see how differently the same little leopard turns out.

When two children want to colour together, give them the same sheet and let them pick colours separately. Have a look halfway through. One might start with the spots while the other is already drawing a place for the leopard to stand. Keep both pictures, even if the two leopards look as though they belong in quite different stories.

You can print a single page and use whatever pencils you have nearby. If a child wants to give No. 57 some company, there’s space around the little leopard to draw another animal. You could even put the sheet next to the picture book when you read it again and see whether it makes your child want to draw another scene.
