Twenty-six-year-old Song Youan falls into a coma after a car crash and wakes at the desolate border between life and death. A man who calls himself River explains that everyone who arrives must face three trials, and three guardians will return them to a fragment of their life to choose again; passing even one trial means returning to the living, otherwise the coma never ends.

This novel for ages 10+ asks what one person would be willing to change about his past.
