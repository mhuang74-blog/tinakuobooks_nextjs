> If life could start over, which memory would you change?
> Tina Kuo, the year's most anticipated author,
> takes on life and death in her latest work that shakes the soul!

This is the desolate border where life and death meet. Only the memories of the past can light your way. Three guardians, three chances to return to the world of the living, and one fantastical journey that turns fate around.

Twenty-six-year-old Song Youan is a rising star of the entertainment world. With a bright future ahead of him, he instead falls into a coma after a car accident. When he opens his eyes again, he finds himself in a strange otherworld: a purple-gray sky stretches without end, there is not a soul around, and all is deathly silent. Just as Song Youan wonders why he has come to this place, a mysterious man who calls himself River suddenly appears and tells him:

Everyone who comes to the memory border must pass through three trials. The three guardians will help them return to a certain fragment of their life, make up for the mistakes they made then, and choose again. Passing even one trial is enough to return to the world of the living; otherwise they will stay in a coma forever.

What secret, unknown to others, lies behind Song Youan's seemingly glamorous life? Which memories will each of the three guardians awaken in him? And will he be able to make the right choices, clear the trials in time, and return to the world of the living?
